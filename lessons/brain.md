<h2>用脑与恢复 · Brain Recovery</h2>
<p class="date">2026/10/10</p>

<p>昨天，我在 Obsidian 里整理了约 264 条收集来的观点，AI 另外处理了 84 条。<br>
<span class="en">Yesterday, I organized about 264 collected viewpoints in Obsidian. AI processed another 84.</span></p>

<p>我改了很多内容，提交了 34 次 commit，也有两次恍然大悟的时刻。<br>
<span class="en">I made many changes and committed 34 times. I also had two moments of "Oh, so that is how it works."</span></p>

<p>凌晨 2点左右，我的头很疼。不是感冒，是处理信息太多带来的疼。我已经没法有效地工作了。<br>
<span class="en">Around 2 a.m., my head started to hurt. It was not a cold. It was the pain of processing too much information. I could no longer work well.</span></p>

<p>就在这时，出现了一次我没有察觉的焦虑。它来自潜意识：头很疼，但我还想像往常一样，继续把事情做完。<br>
<span class="en">At that moment, a quiet kind of anxiety came in, and I did not notice it. It came from my subconscious: my head hurt, but I wanted to keep going, as I always did.</span></p>

<p>这一次，我第一次意识到：继续做，只是看上去把事情做了。<br>
<span class="en">This time, for the first time, I saw it clearly: if I kept going, the work would only look done.</span></p>

<p>疲惫时的做事方式，会带来很细微、很难察觉的误差。<br>
<span class="en">When I am tired, the way I work brings in small errors that are hard to see.</span></p>

<p>比如那个时候，我本该从观点里提炼需要的东西。可我没有看观点，反而去另一张人物表里找。<br>
<span class="en">For example, at that time I should have taken what I needed from the viewpoints. Instead, I skipped them and searched in a different table of people.</span></p>

<p>这不算错，但效率很低，也没有真正解决问题。<br>
<span class="en">This was not wrong. But it was slow, and it did not really solve the problem.</span></p>

<p>这是一条疲惫时下意识走上的小岔路，以前的我也常常这样走。以前的我，肯定注意不到。<br>
<span class="en">It was a small wrong turn that I took without thinking, because I was tired. My old self took turns like this often. My old self would never have noticed.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>所幸，昨晚我第一次停了下来。我去看了一会儿哆啦 A 梦。<br>
<span class="en">Luckily, last night I stopped for the first time. I watched some Doraemon.</span></p>

<p>然后我发语音给 agent：关掉今天的任务，记下大脑疲惫时决策出错的细微表现，任务明天再做。<br>
<span class="en">Then I sent a voice message to my agent: close today's tasks, record the small signs of bad decisions from a tired brain, and do the tasks tomorrow.</span></p>

<p>放在以前，我肯定会熬夜做完。<br>
<span class="en">Before, I would have stayed up all night to finish.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>今早醒来，大脑从头疼中恢复了。然后，过去几天的情绪好像离我远了，我开始看到更多的解法。<br>
<span class="en">This morning, I woke up, and my brain had recovered from the headache. Then the feelings of the past few days seemed far away, and I began to see more solutions.</span></p>

<p>我感觉状态被拉了起来。好好睡觉，是真道理。<br>
<span class="en">I felt my energy come back. Sleep well. It is simple advice, and it is true.</span></p>
