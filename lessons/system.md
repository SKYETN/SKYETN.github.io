<h2>系统改版 · Redesigning My System</h2>
<p class="date">2026/10/03</p>

<p>从 2024 年 12 月 5 号到今天，这套系统换过很多次。<br>
<span class="en">Since December 5, 2024, this system has changed many times.</span></p>

<p>从记事本，到 TickTick，到 Notion，再到现在的 Obsidian 加 Notion。<br>
<span class="en">It went from a paper notebook, to TickTick, to Notion, and now to Obsidian plus Notion.</span></p>

<p>从极简，到繁琐，再到平衡。<br>
<span class="en">From minimal, to heavy, and then to balance.</span></p>

<p>花这么多力气整理，不是每个人都需要。对我来说，它是值得的。<br>
<span class="en">Not everyone needs to spend this much effort on a system. For me, it has been worth it.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>有时我会读历史，看大事件背后的团队和一次次决策。<br>
<span class="en">Sometimes I read history: the teams behind big events, and the decisions they made one by one.</span></p>

<p>那些不太出名、却起了重要作用的人，常常让我印象很深。<br>
<span class="en">The people who were not famous, but still played an important part, often stay with me.</span></p>

<p>世界一直都是群星闪耀的，今天也一样。<br>
<span class="en">The world has always been full of shining stars. It still is today.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>这次改版，来自一个问题：什么样的错误，最容易被平静地修正？<br>
<span class="en">This redesign started with one question: which kind of mistake is the easiest to fix calmly?</span></p>

<p>做计算机和 AI 的人，大多对代码出错很坦然。<br>
<span class="en">People in computer science and AI are usually calm about bugs in their code.</span></p>

<p>训练模型时，loss 不对、梯度出了问题，我们会直接去改。<br>
<span class="en">When the loss looks wrong or the gradient breaks during training, we simply go and fix it.</span></p>

<p>很少有人会因为一个 bug 难过很久。修好它，就继续往前走。<br>
<span class="en">Few people feel bad about a bug for long. You fix it, and you move on.</span></p>

<p>计算机领域对错误很宽容，也愿意把错误公开出来，一起改。<br>
<span class="en">The field is kind to mistakes. People even share them in the open and fix them together.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>于是我想：日常的事情，能不能也当成一个训练过程来看？<br>
<span class="en">So I wondered: could daily work also be seen as a training process?</span></p>

<p>Obsidian 就是日志。遇到的问题，写成一个个可以观察的指标。<br>
<span class="en">Obsidian is the log. Each problem becomes a signal that I can watch.</span></p>

<p>不同的事，节奏不一样：有些像 LSTM，比如会议；有些像 Transformer，比如知识卡片的更新。<br>
<span class="en">Different things move at different speeds. Some are like an LSTM, such as meetings. Some are like a Transformer, such as how my knowledge cards update.</span></p>

<p>我知道，这样的结构会有不确定性。<br>
<span class="en">I know this kind of structure comes with uncertainty.</span></p>

<p>但就像写递归：相信当前这一层返回的是对的，最后的答案也会是对的。<br>
<span class="en">But it is like writing recursion: trust that the current call returns the right result, and the final answer will be right too.</span></p>

<p>建这套系统，是为了让判断少受情绪的影响。<br>
<span class="en">I built this system so that my judgment depends less on my mood.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>我还给文件建了数据库表，有清楚的主键和外键。<br>
<span class="en">I also built database tables for my files, with clear primary keys and foreign keys.</span></p>

<p>结构清楚以后，很多事情变得更容易看清。<br>
<span class="en">Once the structure was clear, many things became easier to see.</span></p>

<p>一个问题，往往只是某个指标需要调整。<br>
<span class="en">A problem is often just one signal that needs a small adjustment.</span></p>

<p>检查，调整，继续，过程顺畅了很多。<br>
<span class="en">Check, adjust, continue. The process became much smoother.</span></p>

<p>也许有经验的人很快就能做到这些。我需要一个脚手架，帮我慢慢练。<br>
<span class="en">Experienced people may do this quickly. I need a scaffold to help me practice, step by step.</span></p>

<p>这个脚手架还能记录我需要多久才能平静下来。看着这个数字慢慢变小，挺有意思的。<br>
<span class="en">The scaffold can even record how long it takes me to calm down. It is fun to watch that number slowly go down.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>在这次整理中，我也学到了一些和 AI 合作的经验。<br>
<span class="en">While doing this, I also learned a few things about working with AI.</span></p>

<p>效率提高了，但也要留意其中的陷阱。<br>
<span class="en">Speed goes up, but there are traps to watch for.</span></p>

<p>AI 给出的往往不是一个确定的答案，而是一些可能性。<br>
<span class="en">AI often gives you possibilities, not one fixed answer.</span></p>

<p>有人喜欢把它限定得很严。我更倾向于给它一定范围内的自由。<br>
<span class="en">Some people prefer tight control. I lean toward giving it some freedom within clear limits.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>整理时我还发现，和 AI 的对话只保留了我的部分，AI 的回复没有保存下来。<br>
<span class="en">I also found that my chats with AI only kept my side. The AI replies were not saved.</span></p>

<p>一共 466 个对话，完整保留 269 个，大约 880 万字，4000 轮。<br>
<span class="en">There were 466 conversations. 269 were fully kept: about 8.8 million characters and 4,000 rounds.</span></p>

<p>一开始觉得可惜。后来想，只留下自己的话，也许更能看出这一路的变化。<br>
<span class="en">At first, it felt like a loss. Later I thought: my own words alone may show the changes along the way even more clearly.</span></p>

<div class="gap"></div>

<p>我想记住这种状态：认真看问题，得到反馈，再慢慢调整。<br>
<span class="en">I want to remember this way of working: look at the problem carefully, get feedback, and adjust slowly.</span></p>

<p>进度像水流，顺着事情发展就好。<br>
<span class="en">Progress is like water. Let it follow the shape of things.</span></p>
